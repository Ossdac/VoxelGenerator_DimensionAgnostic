# Credits

## Stanford Dragon

`Assets/StanfordDragon.obj` is the Stanford Dragon (`dragon_vrip_res3`) from the
[Stanford 3D Scanning Repository](https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/),
courtesy of the Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory. It is used here for
non-commercial purposes, as the repository permits.

It was converted from PLY to OBJ and uniformly scaled. The geometry is otherwise unchanged.
